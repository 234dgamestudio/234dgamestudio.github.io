About 234D Games

234D Games is an independent game studio creating immersive digital experiences through the combination of creativity, technology and innovation. Founded by an experienced digital artist and game developer, the studio brings together extensive expertise in 2D and 3D design, game development, animation, visual effects and interactive technologies. From mobile games to immersive experiences, 234D Games focuses on building engaging worlds, exciting gameplay and memorable digital experiences.

Behind 234D Games

234D Games is an independent game development studio focused on creating engaging mobile games and immersive digital experiences.

The studio's expertise spans 2D and 3D design, game development, animation, visual effects (VFX), Virtual Reality (VR), Augmented Reality (AR), Extended Reality (XR), and AI-assisted creative workflows.

Drawing on over 25 years of experience in digital content creation, 234D Games combines creativity, technical expertise and innovation to deliver experiences across gaming, film, architecture, education, product visualization and immersive simulation.

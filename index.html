  • Aircraft combat and shooting systems
  • Missile targeting and lock-on mechanics
  • Enemy health and damage systems
  • Level progression and multiple themes
  • Mobile UI and safe-area implementation
  • Android build, testing and Google Play deployment
  • AdMob monetization integration
